## Intent

A little paper route-keeper juggles a tiny task, a folded plan and a tangled bug.
It learns to send each new task to a different model, while an unbroken red
thread ties the entire conversation together. The thread is the recurring
visual metaphor and the final payoff.

expressive vector characters and imperfect contours; purposeful gestures and
