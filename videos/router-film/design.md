# Keep the thread — art direction

Original ink-and-paper storybook illustration, for an adult developer audience.
One continuous coral thread is the conversation. It is present in every scene.
A sage paper creature with a coral scarf is the route-keeper; it gestures,
looks at the arriving task and guides the same thread.

Palette: paper #F5EEDD; ink #293936; coral #CA5845; sage #A9BA9E;
mustard #E5B958; pale blue #A7C3C7; pale violet #B6AEC5.
Light neutral washes belong to physical paper objects, not arbitrary gradients.

Typography: original vector monoline hand lettering for all film text, drawn
from reusable glyph paths in source/film.js. Uppercase open counters, generous
spacing and limited copy. Dark ink on pale paper; no low-contrast white labels.
No third-party font binary required by the film or standalone player.

Focal subject: the route-keeper and one arriving task. Edge anchors: a discreet
project name at top left and scene counter at top right. Supporting detail:
route labels attached to actual destinations, three physical task illustrations,
an approval lock with a check. Background: a faint paper weave and printed corner marks.
All essential copy stays between x=120–1800 and y=120–900.

Motion: purposeful anticipation, pointing, eye direction, a scarf that follows
the body, task cards with weighted landings, paths drawing toward destinations,
scene rearrangements and a continuous thread between scenes. Small breathing
motion may support a hold; no whole-frame shake, flashing, or endlessly
bouncing headlines. The final message remains readable for at least 3 seconds.
